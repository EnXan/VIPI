Brand guidelines · 1.0 · October 2026

VIPI finds hidden instructions in images before an AI model reads them. The logo works the same way: at first glance it says VI. Look closer and it reads VIPI.

Name

VIPI is the full name.

VIPI stands on its own and is never spelled out. Always write it in capitals, including in running text: “VIPI scanned 3 images.”

Logo

Two layers on one grid.

The logo sits on a grid of 19 × 7 pixels. VI is built from full pixels. PI sits on the same grid, but each pixel is only 30% of the full edge length. From a distance and at small sizes PI disappears; up close it is readable.

Visible layer · 1 pixel Hidden layer · 0.3 pixel Grid · 19 × 7
Variants

Four forms for every surface.

The logo comes in two colors only: white on dark backgrounds and Ink on light backgrounds. The horizontal logo is the default. The stacked version is for narrow and square spaces.

Clear space

Give it room, keep it large enough.

Keep at least 2 grid pixels clear on every side. One grid pixel is half the width of the I.

Horizontal from 96 px wide, stacked from 48 px. Below that PI is no longer readable, so use the favicon, which shows only VI.

Color

Black, white and the grays in between.

VIPI is monochrome. The logo appears only in Ink or Paper. The grays are for surfaces, lines and secondary text.

Status colors in the product

Only for scan results in the interface. Never in the logo, never as a brand color.

Motion

The scan reveals what is hidden.

A scan line moves from left to right. Each hidden pixel grows to full size as the line reaches it. VIPI appears in full for a moment, then the logo returns to its resting state. 6 seconds in total, looping.
